Tips for parents and teachers

  • Use light cardstock if kids will cut and glue the pieces.
  • Run it as a center activity: one disguise per child, then vote on the best one.
  • Hang the finished turkeys on a bulletin board or the fridge.
  • Ask kids to write a sentence about why their turkey chose that disguise.
