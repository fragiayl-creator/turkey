Crafting Guide

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Get the best results with these simple classroom and family crafting recommendations.

📄

Paper Type Recommendations

Standard copy paper (20-24 lb): Ideal for quick crayon and colored pencil sessions at home or school.

White cardstock (65-80 lb): Highly recommended if kids will be using markers, paint, glue sticks, glitter, feathers, or googly eyes so the paper won't buckle.

🏫

Classroom Activity Ideas

Creative Writing Prompt: Have students write a 3-sentence confession from the turkey explaining why they chose their disguise and how they fooled the farmer.

Book Companion: Pair this activity with beloved Thanksgiving picture books like Turkey Trouble by Wendi Silvano!

🎨

Display & Keepsake Ideas

Hallway Bulletin Board: Create a classroom bulletin board titled "No Turkeys Here, Just Undercover Masters!"

Thanksgiving Dinner Place Cards: Glue popsicle sticks to the back of finished disguises to create cute table centerpieces and personalized place settings.
